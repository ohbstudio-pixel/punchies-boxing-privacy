Privacy Policy

App: Punchies Boxing
Last updated: June 18, 2026

Overview

Punchies Boxing is a mobile boxing game developed for Android. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

Punchies Boxing does not directly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. We do not require an account to play. However, the game displays third-party advertising, and our advertising partners may collect certain information from your device as described below.

Advertising

Punchies Boxing displays advertisements through Unity LevelPlay, the advertising and mediation service provided by Unity Technologies. To serve and measure ads, Unity LevelPlay and its mediated advertising networks may automatically collect and process certain information, which can include:

  • Device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID / Google Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, and similar technical data)
  • IP address and approximate, non-precise location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks, and engagement)

This information is used to deliver and measure advertising, including personalized ads where permitted. We do not control how Unity and its partners use this data. For details on how Unity handles this information, please review Unity's privacy policy:

https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Data Deletion

Since the data collected through advertising is processed by Unity and its partners, you can request deletion of your data by contacting Unity's Data Protection Officer at: DPO@unity3d.com. You may also contact us at the email below and we will assist where possible.

Children's Privacy

Punchies Boxing is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising shown in the game may involve data collection by third parties as described above; we rely on our advertising partners to handle such data in accordance with applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated in the future as need arises.
